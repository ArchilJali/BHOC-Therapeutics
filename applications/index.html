Applications

Where oxygen delivery matters.

BHOC Therapeutics focuses on clinical and translational environments in which oxygen delivery can become a central physiological problem. These areas are fields of scientific and development interest, not approved therapeutic claims.

Emergency & Prehospital

Time-critical oxygen delivery.

Trauma, hemorrhage, EMS, HEMS, remote and humanitarian environments can expose the logistical limits of conventional blood access.

Transplantation

Perfusion and preservation.

Liver, kidney, heart and cross-organ research includes oxygenated perfusion, preservation and viability assessment.

Veterinary

Clinical and regulatory precedent.

Veterinary medicine provides an important evidence base for oxygen-carrier development, including Oxyglobin regulatory history and animal-use literature.

Human Use

Severe anemia and critical care context.

Human-use research spans acute and severe anemia, surgery, cardiovascular stress, critical care and circumstances in which donor blood may be unavailable or unsuitable.

Human-use evidence ↗
Bloodless & Resource-Limited Care

When transfusion is delayed or not an option.

Scientific interest extends to bloodless medicine, resource-limited care, austere medicine and other settings where oxygen-delivery options may need to bridge a temporary gap.

Ischemia & Hypoxia

Supply-demand mismatch.

Ischemia, hypoxia and cardiac emergencies illustrate the difference between oxygen present in circulation and oxygen effectively available to stressed tissue.
