Clinical & Translational Applications

Where oxygen delivery matters.

BHOC Therapeutics focuses on clinical and translational environments in which oxygen delivery can become a central physiological problem. These areas are fields of scientific and development interest, not approved therapeutic claims.

PPH · Maternal Emergency · Blood Access

Every 3 minutes, a mother dies. Every minute, 2 newborns die in the WHO African Region.

These figures describe all maternal and newborn causes, not PPH alone. Severe postpartum hemorrhage nevertheless shows what happens when blood access, emergency transport and definitive care must arrive at the same time.

Clinical context

From emergency logistics to postpartum hemorrhage.

These directions examine where oxygen delivery may become time-critical, difficult to access or difficult to maintain. BHOC is presented as a complementary oxygen-delivery research framework, not as a replacement for blood, transfusion or established clinical care.

EMS / Prehospital

Time-critical oxygen delivery.

Trauma, hemorrhage, EMS, HEMS, battlefield and remote-care systems can face delays in blood access, storage or transport.
