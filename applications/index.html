Priority clinical contexts

PPH · Global Humanitarian Crisis

The crisis is real.
The numbers are not just statistics.

Postpartum hemorrhage is a time-critical blood-access emergency. When help does not arrive in time, mothers and families pay the price.

1 maternal death / 3 minWHO African RegionApproximately 20 maternal deaths every hour
2 newborn deaths / minWHO African RegionApproximately 120 newborn deaths every hour
178,000maternal deaths / yearWHO African Region estimate
1 millionnewborn deaths / yearWHO African Region estimate

These WHO African Region context figures describe maternal and newborn mortality overall-not PPH alone. Estimates vary by source, country and year. BHOC relevance remains a research hypothesis about complementary oxygen delivery-not an approved PPH treatment claim.

EMS · Prehospital · Remote Care

The bleeding starts before the hospital.
So does the oxygen-delivery crisis.

Trauma, hemorrhage and distance create a critical window between oxygen need and definitive care.

A bridge solution for the minutes when donor blood is delayed, unavailable or difficult to deploy.
