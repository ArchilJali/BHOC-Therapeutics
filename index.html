RBC

A highly evolved biological oxygen-delivery system. Effective function depends on physiological temperature, pressure, regulated blood flow, membrane integrity, 2,3-DPG and other cellular cofactors.

Blood is a wider biological system.

BHOC

Cell-free oxygen carrier. No red-cell membrane or full RBC cofactor system required. Designed to support oxygen delivery beyond conventional cellular constraints.
