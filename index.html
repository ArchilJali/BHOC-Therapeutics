The Science

From oxygen in the lungs to oxygen at the cell.

Effective oxygen delivery depends on more than a hemoglobin number. Oxygen loading, blood flow, hemoglobin oxygen affinity, microcirculation, unloading, diffusion and tissue demand all influence whether sufficient oxygen ultimately reaches the cell.

The Technology

Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carriers

BHOC — Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier — describes a platform approach centered on hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport outside the conventional red-cell framework.

The broader scientific field includes HBOCs, historical products such as Oxyglobin and Hemopure, and emerging oxygen-carrier technologies. These technologies differ substantially in source, molecular architecture, oxygen affinity, circulation characteristics, oxidation profile, storage and intended clinical use.

Where Oxygen Delivery Matters

Oxygen-critical clinical environments

BHOC Therapeutics is building a scientific and development framework around conditions in which oxygen delivery can become a central clinical problem. These areas are presented as fields of scientific and development interest, not as approved therapeutic claims.

Emergency, Prehospital & Humanitarian

Time-critical oxygen delivery where blood logistics, geography and access can constrain emergency resuscitation.

Acute & Severe Anemia

Conditions in which blood oxygen-carrying capacity is critically reduced or compatible blood is unavailable.

Surgery, PPH & Bloodless Resuscitation

Surgical blood loss, postpartum hemorrhage and care pathways where transfusion may be delayed or not an option.

Organ Transplantation, Perfusion & Preservation

Kidney, liver and heart environments where oxygenated perfusion and organ preservation influence graft viability.

Ischemia, Hypoxia & Cardiac Emergencies

Scientific context for impaired oxygen delivery in ischemia, hypoxia, myocardial infarction and cardiac arrest.

Sickle Cell Disease & Sickle Cell Anemia

Research context for oxygen delivery, severe anemia and transfusion support in SCD and SCA.

Veterinary Medicine

Scientific, clinical and regulatory evidence from veterinary oxygen-carrier use.

Remote & Resource-Limited Care

Oxygen-delivery options for austere, disaster, battlefield and low-resource healthcare environments.
