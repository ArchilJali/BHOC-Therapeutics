The Architecture

Blood is a system. Oxygen delivery is a function.

Whole blood performs many functions. BHOC focuses on one defined function: hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport beyond the conventional red-cell framework. The comparison below is a scientific framing, not a claim that one platform replaces the other.

RBC

Red blood cell architecture

  • Blood-group compatibility remains relevant to transfusion.
  • Cellular oxygen delivery depends on physiologic temperature, pressure, flow and intracellular cofactors.
  • Red cells operate as part of the wider system of whole blood.
BHOC

Cell-free oxygen-delivery direction

  • Hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport outside the conventional red-cell architecture.
  • Platform direction: compatible with all blood types for all species.
  • Studied as a complementary oxygen-delivery bridge, not a replacement for blood.
Different architecture. The same biological need: oxygen at the tissue and mitochondrial level.

The Technology

Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carriers.

BHOC describes a platform approach centered on hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport outside the conventional red-cell framework, connecting established HBOC science with a new Precision Oxygenation Therapeutics direction.

From oxygen carrying to oxygen-delivery design.

The field includes HBOCs, historical products such as Oxyglobin and Hemopure, and emerging oxygen-carrier technologies. They differ in source, molecular architecture, oxygen affinity, circulation characteristics, oxidation profile, storage and intended use.
