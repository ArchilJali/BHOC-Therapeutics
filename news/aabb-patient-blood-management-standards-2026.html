AABB Proposes New Patient Blood Management Standards for Technology and Safety Oversight

AABB's proposed sixth edition shows how technology verification, AI oversight and continuous safety review are moving into the centre of modern blood and biotherapy programs.

13 September 2026 · Research update

On 11 September 2026, AABB opened public comment on the proposed sixth edition of its Standards for a Patient Blood Management Program. The comment period remains open through 10 November 2026.

The proposed draft addresses monitoring of critical technology infrastructure, defined checks that systems are working as intended, and the use, risk management, verification of output and monitoring of artificial intelligence in an accredited facility. It also addresses informed consent for patients who lack decisional capacity and review of deviations, nonconformances and adverse events for potential safety trends.

Quality systems are part of the therapy.New blood and biotherapy programs need traceable technology, controlled decisions and continuous safety monitoring.

This is a proposed standard, not a final requirement and not evidence for BHOC. For future Precision Oxygen Therapeutics development, it is a useful reference for designing verification, governance, monitoring and safety review from the beginning.
