The Silent Interval in Kidney Transplantation

A donor kidney is preserved, transported and assessed before it can be transplanted. What information about that interval should teams record, and what should patients and care partners be able to see?

17 November 2026BHOC TherapeuticsVersion 1 preprint
The Silent Interval: Organ Perfusion Data, Patient Rights, and the Case for Advocacy-Led Governance of Organ Preservation

Jeff A. Parke and Archil Jaliashvili · Preprints.org · Posted 13 November 2026
DOI 10.20944/preprints202611.1378.v1
Read the preprint

What belongs in the transplant record?

In their second joint preprint, Jeff A. Parke of Cold Ischemia Foundation and Archil Jaliashvili propose common standards for recording preservation time and machine perfusion measurements. They also propose that key information be added to the transplant record and that patient representatives have a formal role in oversight.

The first paper looked at dialysis, organ scarcity and barriers to access. This concept paper focuses on the period between donor kidney recovery and the decision to use the kidney for transplantation.

Continuing the discussion on organ preservation

In an earlier LinkedIn discussion started by Jeff, Jacques Lefebvre, Jefferson Hare, Chris Thome and David Perchinsky and Archil brought different perspectives to the discussion of donor kidney preservation. Jeff also tagged Carl W. Rausch in the original post.
