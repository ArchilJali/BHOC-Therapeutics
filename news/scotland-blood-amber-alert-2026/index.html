Scotland’s Blood Amber Alert and the Case for Upgrading the Blood System

Scotland’s blood shortage adds to the case for upgrading how health systems support blood function. Strengthening donation and supply goes hand in hand with developing complementary ways to support oxygen delivery.

10 November 2026By Archil JaliashviliSource notice: 5 November 2026

What Scotland reported

On 5 November 2026, NHS Fife reported that the Scottish National Blood Transfusion Service had issued a national Amber Alert. Stocks had fallen amid fewer donors and increased use of group O blood. Staffing and weather disruptions had also affected some collection sessions. The service called for donors to book appointments, especially those with O negative and O positive blood.

Where Patient Blood Management can go further

The alert is another reason to upgrade the blood system. Patient Blood Management (PBM) puts the patient’s own blood first. We propose using that framework to strengthen access to compatible donor blood and develop complementary forms of functional support.

We see functional support as the next step for PBM. Donor blood remains essential. When compatible red cells are delayed or unavailable, complementary approaches to supporting oxygen delivery deserve development and evaluation. That is where our Precision Oxygen Therapeutics research fits.
