BHOC Transplant Opens Advocacy Voices & Field Notes

Listening to the people this work is for, sharing their questions and answering them openly.

1 October 2026BHOC TherapeuticsCommunity perspectives

BHOC Transplant has opened Advocacy Voices & Field Notes, a section for patient and care partner perspectives on oxygen delivery and transplantation.

The first featured voice is Jeff Parke, founder and executive director of Cold Ischemia Foundation. His Foundation has published an introduction to BHOC and a series of independent Field Notes.

Clearer information begins with listening

People waiting for a transplant, people living with one and those caring for them should be able to tell us what they need to understand. Their experience helps us explain the work more clearly and ask questions that matter to them.

We welcome questions and suggestions. What is unclear? What would you like to know? What should we explain better?
