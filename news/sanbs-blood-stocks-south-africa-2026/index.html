SANBS Blood Stocks Reported at 4.2 Days of Cover

A dated snapshot of blood availability in South Africa, where SANBS aims to maintain five days of stock for each blood group.

10 October 2026Archil JaliashviliSource report: 9 October 2026

Blood stocks at the South African National Blood Service (SANBS) were reported at 4.2 days of cover in a 9 October 2026 report published by Quicknews. SANBS states that it aims to maintain five days of stock for each blood group.

The service is asking eligible donors to give blood and aims to collect approximately 3,500 units daily. The appeal accompanied the launch of its documentary Why Blood Matters.

Why the reserve matters

Maintaining a national supply requires continuous collection, testing, processing, storage and distribution. A reserve figure describes supply at a given time; it does not show whether a particular compatible unit is available where a patient needs it.

The reported stock level does not establish that hospitals have stopped transfusions or postponed procedures. It does show why blood availability needs regular attention, even within an established national service.
