14 Oct 2026Scientific evidence · Oxygen transport efficiency

27 oxygen-transport publications added after a 30-paper review

Added 22 publications to Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation and 5 to Related Scientific Articles. Three were already published, including Vadapalli, Goldman & Popel (2002). The research compares red blood cells and extracellular hemoglobin carriers by actual oxygen transport, rather than hemoglobin concentration alone.

Six directions cover capillary models, RBC and extracellular hemoglobin comparisons, P50 and cooperativity, facilitated diffusion, low hematocrit and tissue hypoxia, and oxygen distribution with vascular effects. Seven animal studies are also available in Veterinary search. Counts and search records are synchronized.

14 Oct 2026Scientific evidence · Oxygen transport modelling

Capillary oxygen transport modelling added to scientific evidence

One verified publication added: Vadapalli, Goldman & Popel (2002), PMID 12066873, DOI 10.1081/BIO-120004338. The Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation catalogue increased from 215 to 216 records. The research direction compares red blood cells and extracellular hemoglobin oxygen carriers by actual oxygen transport, rather than hemoglobin concentration alone. Related literature is being reviewed for thematic classification and deduplication; it is not included in this addition count. This mathematical study does not establish clinical BHOC efficacy.
