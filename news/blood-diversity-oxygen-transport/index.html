Blood: One Biological System, Extraordinary Diversity

Archil Jaliashvili · 26 November 2026

Two people with the same ABO blood group can differ across many other blood antigens. That diversity raises one question about compatibility and another about function: how does blood deliver oxygen to tissues?

What does the estimate of 2.59 × 10134 represent?

It is the number of possible antigen profiles in an illustrative model combining red-cell, platelet and neutrophil antigens under simplifying assumptions. The calculation does not establish how often those profiles occur or predict whether a transfusion will be compatible.

How does this relate to oxygen delivery?

Hemoglobin binds oxygen in the lungs and releases it in tissues. BHOC research focuses on whether an engineered hemoglobin carrier can support that specific transport function.
