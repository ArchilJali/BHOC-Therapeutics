US Prehospital Blood, AABB Standards and Tissue Oxygenation

BHOC Therapeutics | 12 October 2026

NHTSA, on behalf of FICEMS, requests input on nationwide prehospital blood transfusion by 5 November 2026. Independently, AABB has proposed a second edition of its prehospital and out-of-hospital transfusion standards.

What happens before blood arrives?

Perfusion and oxygen diffusion are related but distinct. Restoring blood flow or raising hemoglobin does not necessarily restore oxygen availability to threatened tissues. BHOC Therapeutics advocates considering tissue hypoxia as a distinct, measurable target for research in severe hemorrhage, shock and myocardial ischemia. Timely reperfusion remains essential in myocardial infarction, and excessive oxygen can also be harmful. No clinical efficacy or regulatory authorization for the BHOC platform is implied.
