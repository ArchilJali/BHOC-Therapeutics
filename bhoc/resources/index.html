One architecture, no repetition

What belongs where.

BHOC is the definition, orientation and conceptual map. Science explains biological mechanism. Technology explains how the oxygen-carrying function is engineered. Evidence shows what is supported and where to verify it. BHOC-platform is the deep source-linked repository. BHOC Veterinary owns species-specific animal-health and biodiversity pathways.

Each deeper page should answer one question well and link back here. This keeps terminology consistent, avoids duplicated claims and gives BHOC one clear definition and orientation hub.

Historical Evolution

Evolution of Blood Transfusion and Oxygen Therapeutics.

For centuries, medicine removed blood to treat disease. Then it learned to replace blood to save life, identify compatibility, build donor systems and search for artificial blood and blood substitutes. The history of HBOCs, Hemopure, HBOC-201, Oxyglobin, Biopure, Perftoran and newer oxygen-carrier applications shows how the scientific question gradually moved from replacing blood as a whole toward understanding and supporting specific biological functions.

The full article follows the main steps from early transfusion and Landsteiner's blood-group discovery to blood banking, HIV-era safety, artificial oxygen carriers, HBOC development, donor-system constraints, modern EMS and the BHOC function-first framework.
