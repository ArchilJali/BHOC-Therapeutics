Historical question

Artificial Blood & Blood Substitute: what did we learn?

These terms matter because they shaped the field, public expectations and search behavior. They are retained here as historical and discovery bridges, not as the current BHOC product definition.

In development

HBOC means hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier, the established scientific term used across decades of literature. Artificial blood and blood substitute are older and still widely searched terms, but they can imply replacement of the many functions of blood. BHOC Therapeutics retains these expressions for historical, scientific and search discovery while using Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier and Precision Oxygen Therapeutics for current positioning.

Historical lesson

The label was broader than the biological function.

The field's history is not something to hide. It is part of understanding why modern oxygen-carrier development must separate class labels from product-specific design, physiology, indication, evidence and benefit-risk.

The semantic pathway is therefore:

Artificial blood→Blood substitute→HBOC→BHOC→Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

The source-linked historical timeline preserves the broader story from transfusion and blood banking through artificial oxygen-carrier research, Biopure, Hemopure, HBOC-201, Oxyglobin and the scientific lessons that followed.
