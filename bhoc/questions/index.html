BHOC questions and answers

A short entry point to the definition, the historical HBOC terminology and the supporting sources.

What does BHOC mean?

BHOC means Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier. BHOC Therapeutics uses it for a development framework focused on characterizing and controlling hemoglobin-mediated oxygen delivery.

Read the full definition →

How does BHOC relate to HBOC?

HBOC means hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier, the established historical field term. BHOC identifies the Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier framework within that field. It does not replace HBOC as a scientific or regulatory term.

See the terminology and history →

What is Precision Oxygen Therapeutics?

It is a proposed framework for investigating where, when and how oxygen delivery could be supported, measured and controlled according to tissue and clinical context.

Read the framework →

Does the BHOC framework mean there is an approved BHOC treatment?

No. The framework and source-linked research material do not establish a BHOC clinical indication, product approval or treatment recommendation. Historical HBOC product records and external research must be interpreted separately.
