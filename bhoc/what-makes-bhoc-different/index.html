BHOC framework

What makes BHOC different?

BHOC is the definition, orientation and conceptual map. It connects hemoglobin biology to product-specific oxygen-delivery development without claiming to reproduce every function of blood.

Biology→Function→Technology→Physiology→Evidence→Application

Science explains biological mechanism. Technology explains how the oxygen-carrying function is engineered. Evidence shows what is supported and where to verify it. BHOC-platform is the deep source-linked repository. BHOC Veterinary owns species-specific animal-health and biodiversity pathways.

Each deeper page should answer one question well and link back here. This keeps terminology consistent, avoids duplicated claims and gives BHOC one clear definition and orientation hub.
