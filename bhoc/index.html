Understand BHOC.

BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier) is a cell-free hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier designed to support tissue oxygen delivery. BHOC Therapeutics develops this approach within Precision Oxygen Therapeutics. Its purpose is oxygen transport when red-cell oxygen delivery is insufficient, not replacement of all the functions of blood.

Blood is a system.Oxygen delivery is a function.
