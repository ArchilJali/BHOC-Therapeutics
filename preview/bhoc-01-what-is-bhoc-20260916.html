What is BHOC?

One defined biological function.

Hemoglobin is the principal oxygen-carrying protein in red blood cells. A hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier moves that oxygen-carrying function outside the intact red-cell framework through a specifically engineered hemoglobin formulation.

Core definition

BHOC builds on the scientific history of hemoglobin-based oxygen carriers (HBOCs) while emphasizing biological function, tissue oxygen delivery, evidence and practical deployment. BHOC is not presented as a substitute for every function of blood.

FOUNDATION

Hemoglobin

The biological molecule that reversibly binds oxygen and provides the core oxygen-carrying function used in hemoglobin-based oxygen-carrier technologies.

FIELD

HBOC

Hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier: the established literature term covering multiple molecular designs, generations, products and development programs.

BHOC FRAMEWORK

Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier

A function-first framing used by BHOC Therapeutics to connect hemoglobin biology, oxygen delivery, evidence, manufacturing and application-specific development.
