## BHOC acronym and entity disambiguation strategy

### Problem confirmed 4 September 2026

The bare acronym `BHOC` is ambiguous in public search. An unrelated entity, **Behavioral Healthcare Outcomes Consortium**, owns a strong exact-match LinkedIn URL `/company/bhoc/` and can rank prominently for the naked acronym. This is not our Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier entity.

Third-party posts about our BHOC can also outrank Archil Jaliashvili's own posts because older indexed URLs may have stronger crawl history, engagement and external references. This is an authority/distribution issue, not evidence that Google fails to index Archil.

### Strategic implication

Do not use the naked query `BHOC` as the only short-term SEO KPI. Track it, but first strengthen the semantic entity that Google must associate with our work:

**Archil Jaliashvili -> BHOC -> Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier -> HBOC -> hemoglobin-mediated oxygen delivery -> Precision Oxygen Therapeutics**

The goal is broader than outranking any one person or page. We want multiple owned and independent authority surfaces to appear across the complete BHOC/HBOC/oxygen-carrier search cluster.

## Blood substitute / artificial blood search-bridge strategy

`blood substitute` and `artificial blood` are established public and scientific search terms with decades of literature, but they are imprecise for technologies whose primary function is oxygen transport rather than replacement of whole blood.

Desired semantic bridge:

**blood substitute / artificial blood -> hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier (HBOC) -> BHOC / Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier -> Precision Oxygen Therapeutics**

Ownership rules:

1. Do **not** change the BHOC homepage primary intent to `blood substitute` or `artificial blood`.
2. `/bhoc/` is the central corporate definition and terminology page. It should own the broad bridge between BHOC, HBOC, artificial blood, blood substitute, hemoglobin, Hemopure/HBOC-201, Oxyglobin and Precision Oxygen Therapeutics.
3. `/technology/` supports that page with a concise corrective explanation and a descriptive internal link to `/bhoc/`.
4. `/science/` owns physiology, hemoglobin-mediated oxygen delivery, tissue oxygenation and metabolism, and should link to `/bhoc/` for terminology rather than duplicate it.
5. `/evidence/` owns source-linked BHOC evidence and should link to `/bhoc/` for definition/terminology and to BHOC-platform for deeper sources.
6. `BHOC-platform` owns deeper historical/evidence intent for generic searches such as `blood substitute HBOC`, `artificial blood HBOC`, `Hemopure blood substitute`, Oxyglobin regulatory history and the evolution of oxygen-carrier terminology.
7. A future BHOC-platform terminology/history page may provide the deeper evidence layer, but it must not duplicate the corporate `/bhoc/` page. Its purpose would be citations, historical sources and evidence chronology.
8. Do not keyword-stuff `blood substitute` or `artificial blood` across unrelated application pages, veterinary pages or homepage sections.

## Hemoglobin search strategy

Do not treat the naked word `hemoglobin` as a realistic standalone ownership target. It is an extremely broad biological and clinical entity dominated by major reference, medical and academic sources.

Use hemoglobin to strengthen the relevant functional cluster instead:

**hemoglobin -> hemoglobin oxygen carrier -> hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier -> HBOC -> Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier -> BHOC -> oxygen delivery / tissue oxygenation**

Priority combinations include `hemoglobin oxygen carrier`, `hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier`, `hemoglobin oxygen delivery`, `cell-free hemoglobin oxygen carrier`, `hemoglobin therapeutics`, `Archil Jaliashvili hemoglobin oxygen carrier`, `Hemopure hemoglobin oxygen carrier` and `Oxyglobin hemoglobin oxygen carrier`.

The desired entity relationship is:

**Archil Jaliashvili -> BHOC -> Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier -> HBOC -> oxygen delivery / tissue oxygenation -> legacy evidence such as Oxyglobin, Hemopure, Biopure and HBOC-201.**

Legacy-search extension:

Rules:

1. Important BHOC pages should use descriptive links to Archil where natural, not generic anchors such as `LinkedIn` alone.
2. Strong evidence/history pages authored by Archil should retain clear author metadata and structured data.
3. Important LinkedIn posts should place the relevant semantic phrase in the first one or two lines where it still sounds natural. Example concepts: `BHOC - Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier`, `HBOC and BHOC`, `Hemopure / HBOC-201`, `Oxyglobin and veterinary HBOC evidence`, or `Artificial blood and blood substitute - why the terminology evolved`.
4. Selected posts should deliberately connect Archil's name and voice to one specific cluster at a time. Do not place every keyword into every post.
5. For legacy-language SEO, use `blood substitute` and/or `artificial blood` naturally in selected posts, then immediately distinguish the term from current BHOC positioning.
6. Do not rewrite every post for SEO and do not keyword-stuff. The goal is entity consistency, not repetitive copy.
7. External mentions by Hemostasis Today, clinicians, scientists, journals and independent professionals are authority surfaces. Preserve and reinforce them with relevant cross-links where appropriate rather than trying to duplicate their content.
8. Third-party pages that rank above Archil should be treated as distribution benchmarks and possible authority signals, not automatically as negative competitors.
