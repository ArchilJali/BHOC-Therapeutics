## BHOC acronym and entity disambiguation strategy

### Problem confirmed 4 September 2026

The bare acronym `BHOC` is ambiguous in public search. An unrelated entity, **Behavioral Healthcare Outcomes Consortium**, owns a strong exact-match LinkedIn URL `/company/bhoc/` and can rank prominently for the naked acronym. This is not our Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier entity.

Third-party posts about our BHOC, including Mary Kiige's LinkedIn content, can also outrank Archil Jaliashvili's own posts because older indexed URLs may have stronger crawl history, engagement and external references. This is an authority/distribution issue, not evidence that Google fails to index Archil.

### Strategic implication

Do not use the naked query `BHOC` as the only short-term SEO KPI. Track it, but first strengthen the semantic entity that Google must associate with our work:

**BHOC = Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier**

The near-term goal is to dominate oxygen/hemoglobin-qualified BHOC searches before treating the bare acronym as the main battleground.

## Personal authority strategy - Archil Jaliashvili

The desired entity relationship is:

**Archil Jaliashvili -> BHOC -> Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier -> HBOC -> oxygen delivery / tissue oxygenation -> legacy evidence such as Oxyglobin, Hemopure, Biopure and HBOC-201.**

Rules:

1. Important BHOC pages should use descriptive links to Archil where natural, not generic anchors such as `LinkedIn` alone.
2. Strong evidence/history pages authored by Archil should retain clear author metadata and structured data.
3. Important LinkedIn posts should place the relevant semantic phrase in the first one or two lines where it still sounds natural. Example concept: `BHOC - Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier` or `HBOC and BHOC in prehospital oxygen delivery`.
4. Do not rewrite every post for SEO and do not keyword-stuff. The goal is entity consistency, not repetitive copy.
5. External mentions by Hemostasis Today, Mary Kiige, clinicians, scientists and other independent professionals are authority surfaces. Preserve and reinforce them with relevant cross-links where appropriate rather than trying to duplicate their content.
6. When a third-party BHOC post ranks above Archil, treat it as a distribution benchmark and possible authority signal, not automatically as a negative competitor.
