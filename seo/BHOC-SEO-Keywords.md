## 1. Purpose

This document defines the master English-language SEO vocabulary for:

- `bhoctherapeutics.com` — corporate, strategic, application, market and partnership content;
- `ArchilJali/BHOC-platform` — CORE/VERIFIED evidence, publications, regulatory history and primary documents.

The two resources must support each other without duplicating complete pages or competing for the same primary search intent.

## 2. Positioning and terminology rules

### Preferred positioning

- BHOC = **Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier**
- **Precision Oxygenation Therapeutics**
- **Blood is a system. Oxygen delivery is a function.**
- BHOC is positioned as an additional oxygen-delivery option and potential bridge where conventional blood access or function is limited.

### Do not use as BHOC positioning

- artificial blood
- blood substitute
- replacement for blood
- replacement for blood donors

These expressions may appear only when accurately describing historical language, competitor positioning or a quoted source.

### Terms excluded from this SEO architecture

- tissue-level oxygenation
- microvascular oxygenation

### English corrections

- Use **blood transfusion**, not “blood transplantation”.
- Use **organ transplantation** for transplant medicine.
- Use **heart attack**, **myocardial infarction** and **acute myocardial infarction**, not “hard attack” or “inpoharct”.
- Do not target `SCA` alone. Spell out **sickle cell anemia (SCA)** or **sudden cardiac arrest (SCA)** according to the page context.

## 9. On-page implementation rule

For each indexable page select:

- one primary keyword;
- three to five secondary keywords;
- five to ten supporting semantic terms;
- one unique page title;
- one unique H1;
- one unique meta description;
- one clear internal-link anchor from another page;
- descriptive image alt text where appropriate.

Place the primary phrase naturally in:

- the `<title>`;
- the H1;
- the opening paragraph;
- at least one H2 when natural;
- the meta description;
- the page URL where practical;
- one or more relevant internal links.

Do not use invisible keyword blocks, repetitive lists in public page text, or keyword stuffing. A keyword list is a planning tool, not page copy.
