The work is organized around a clear definition: BHOC means Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier. The framework places oxygen delivery, tissue physiology, evidence traceability and translational context before therapeutic claims.

BHOC Therapeutics is the official company-facing site. The BHOC Scientific Evidence Hub maintains source-linked scientific, clinical, regulatory and historical material. BHOC Veterinary organizes veterinary and species-specific context.
