The decision in the case

Two markets, one launch question.

The publisher places the case in early 1998: veterinary-market Oxyglobin had received approval, while human-market Hemopure was still being tested. The management question was whether to launch Oxyglobin immediately or wait. A concern was that the veterinary product's price could shape expectations for the later human product, whose approval remained uncertain.

Why it belongs in this archive

Scientific development is only part of a technology's history. This case adds a commercial perspective: how to introduce related products into different markets while regulatory and pricing questions remain open.
