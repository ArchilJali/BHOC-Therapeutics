Human registry records · Hemopure / HBOC-201

Hemopure: registered human clinical studies

Official ClinicalTrials.gov records for Hemopure, HBOC-201 and hemoglobin glutamer-250 (bovine). Registry records are listed separately from journal publications.

How many records were found?

Nine human records were found: six Interventional records and three Expanded Access records. Five of the six interventional records are Phase 2; the sixth is classified as Interventional with phase Not Applicable, despite Expanded Access Study in its title. Four of the five Phase 2 records are randomized. These are not nine randomized controlled trials.

Oxyglobin is a different veterinary formulation, hemoglobin glutamer-200 / HBOC-301. No ClinicalTrials.gov records were found for the four checked Oxyglobin aliases. Read the separate veterinary registry and FDA/EMA source note →

Registry search checked 31 October 2026. Each record below retains its own last-update and status-verification dates. Registration does not establish efficacy, approval or current access.

Questions and search scope

Are these Oxyglobin trials?

No. The nine human records concern Hemopure / HBOC-201 / hemoglobin glutamer-250. Oxyglobin is the veterinary glutamer-200 formulation. Evidence for either historical product is not proof of a BHOC product's safety, efficacy or approval.

Does Unknown mean Recruiting?

No. NCT00301483 and NCT00301535 have Unknown current status. Recruiting is their last known status from 2008. NCT01881503 lists Enrolling by invitation, with status last verified in August 2021. Confirm any present access directly with the responsible study team.

Are results posted in the registry?

None of these nine records had structured results posted at the check date. This does not establish that journal articles are absent. A publication is linked to an NCT record only after its protocol and participant cohort have been matched.

Are expanded access and marketing approval the same?

No. Expanded access concerns investigational treatment under the relevant regulatory pathway. It is not a marketing approval. Read the FDA expanded-access source note.

How was this list compiled?

The dated API audit checks Oxyglobin, HBOC-301, HBOC-200, hemoglobin glutamer-200, Hemopure, HBOC-201 and hemoglobin glutamer-250, plus a broader Biopure query. The broader query includes unrelated products, which were excluded. This is a product-specific ClinicalTrials.gov search, not an inventory of every historical human HBOC protocol or every registry worldwide.
