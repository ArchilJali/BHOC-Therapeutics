Record checklist

What each record needs.

  • Original DOI or PubMed record, authors and publication date.
  • Organ, study design, population, intervention and endpoint.
  • Finding and limitations stated at the scope of that study.
  • Corrections and source status kept with the record.
Collection history

Five related records came from the central BHOC catalogue on 26 September 2026. Two kidney xenotransplant studies were added on 27 September. Correction notices are not counted as studies.

A human kidney perfusion study of hypoxic RBC storage was added on 3 October 2026. It is classified as external ex vivo organ evidence, with a separate functional-quality brief.

These sources inform research. They do not establish BHOC efficacy, product equivalence or a clinical transplant indication.
