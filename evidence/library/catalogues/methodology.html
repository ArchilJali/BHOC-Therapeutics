About the evidence catalogue

Find publications across oxygen therapeutics, oxygen physiology and relevant clinical research.

Three topics, one publication record

Each publication has one primary scientific catalogue. Repeated entries are reconciled using DOI, PMID and bibliographic comparisons. Application searches draw from the same publication records and can overlap.

How the statistics work

Publication totals count unique records. Species, model and therapeutic groups follow the recorded metadata. Author institutions count indexed affiliation names; journal abbreviations and corresponding full names are combined. Missing labels are excluded from these counts. Statistics on search pages update with the active filters.

Read findings in context

Clinical, preclinical, analytical and review evidence should be interpreted at publication level. Historical product findings and studies of oxygen physiology do not establish BHOC efficacy, safety or regulatory approval. Author affiliations do not imply institutional endorsement or identify a confirmed study location.

Available DOI, PubMed and original source links support source checking. A supplied identifier does not mean that every bibliographic field or study conclusion has been independently verified. Incomplete source entries are excluded from public search.

The displayed total is the sum of Transplant, Human, Veterinary, Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation, and Related Scientific Articles: 62 + 180 + 260 + 210 + 206 = 918 entries. A publication appears once in each applicable collection, so this total includes repeated placements across collections.
