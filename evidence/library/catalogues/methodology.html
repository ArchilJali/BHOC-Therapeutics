Duplicate and metadata handling

Matching DOI, PMID and normalized title/year connect source occurrences. Title, author, journal and citation comparisons resolve missing years and transcription variants. DOI URLs are displayed only when a usable identifier is retained; truncated identifiers remain in source provenance. Conflicting metadata are resolved in favour of existing identified or curated source records.

The two canine intestinal-perfusion records PMID 11575346 and PMID 28404246 remain separate: their journals and identifiers differ. Similar titles alone are not sufficient to merge distinct publications.

Examples of corrected source metadata include a page number misread as a publication year, an access date used as a publication year, and a title attached to the wrong citation. The original source occurrence remains available in each record. The incomplete parasite-induced anaemia source remains explicitly unverified.

Evidence boundaries

Historical product evidence, mechanistic research and possible future BHOC applications are distinct. Inclusion is not a clinical recommendation or proof of BHOC efficacy. A supplied DOI or PMID is a source identifier; incomplete or unverified records require bibliographic review.
