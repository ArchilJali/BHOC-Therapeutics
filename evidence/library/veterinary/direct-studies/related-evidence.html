Historical clinical adoption & veterinary education

Secondary evidence · not included in the six-study count
University textbook / historical clinical adoption2010/2011

HBOC/Oxyglobin in Sıvı Sağaltımı [Fluid Therapy]

Author
Prof. Dr. Tarık Bilal
Institution
Istanbul University Faculty of Veterinary Medicine
Evidence type
university veterinary textbook

A dedicated section, “Hemoglobin Bazlı Oksijen Taşıyıcılar (HBOC)” [Hemoglobin-Based Oxygen Carriers], documents that HBOC/Oxyglobin had entered formal veterinary education and clinical fluid-therapy discussion. The text describes Oxyglobin as purified polymerized bovine hemoglobin used to increase oxygen-carrying capacity and discusses it in relation to hemolytic anemia, acute intravascular volume loss, hemorrhagic shock and perioperative fluid management.

Physiological point: volume replacement ≠ oxygen transport. Restoring circulating volume and restoring oxygen-carrying capacity are distinct therapeutic functions.

Statements reported in the textbook: in its discussion of hemorrhagic shock, the book states that Oxyglobin is “10 times more effective than blood”. Elsewhere it states that “1 mL Oxyglobin binds an amount of oxygen equivalent to 3 mL of blood.”

PRIMARY SOURCE VERIFICATION PENDING. These quantitative statements are reported here specifically as claims made in the textbook, not as independently validated BHOC-VET conclusions. The cited primary sources must be traced to determine whether the comparison concerns oxygen content, effective oxygen delivery, plasma distribution, microcirculatory delivery, or clinical outcome. The “10 times more effective than blood” statement particularly requires primary-source verification before use as an efficacy claim.
