# Veterinary related-evidence method

A related record is a source-linked publication, professional guidance or comparator relevant to veterinary oxygen-delivery research. It is not BHOC-specific efficacy evidence. The current nine-record dataset retains each source's original title, design, species, DOI or primary URL, evidence role and verification label.

- **Published Evidence:** A finding reported by the named study, guideline or regulator. The named product, species, intervention, outcome and limitations travel with the claim.
- **Derived Analysis:** A calculation or comparison from published numbers. Show inputs and method; do not relabel it as a pooled treatment effect.
- **Scientific Perspective:** Author interpretation across sources, distinct from their findings.
- **Research Hypothesis:** A testable proposal requiring its own preclinical, safety and product-specific evidence.

Professional reviews without a DOI remain labelled secondary. Historical Oxyglobin/HBOC publications and FDA/EMA history are catalogued in the [central veterinary library](https://bhoctherapeutics.com/evidence/library/veterinary/Vet-index.html). One publication should have one active master record; other pages link to it and preserve its source identifiers.

This method was condensed from the former Oxygen Delivery Evidence repository's evidence methodology and taxonomy (source commit `50c66ceff457a2b6505b3889446b9b24bd42829b`).
