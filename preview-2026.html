The Technology

Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carriers.

BHOC describes a platform approach centered on hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport outside the conventional red-cell framework, connecting established HBOC science with a new Precision Oxygenation Therapeutics direction.

From oxygen carrying to oxygen-delivery design.

The field includes HBOCs, historical products such as Oxyglobin and Hemopure, and emerging oxygen-carrier technologies. They differ in source, molecular architecture, oxygen affinity, circulation characteristics, oxidation profile, storage and intended use.
