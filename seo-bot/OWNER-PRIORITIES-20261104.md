## Mandatory page-level registry

Maintain one row per intended public canonical page, including each standalone News item, scientific page, application page, question page and catalogue. Discover pages from both sitemaps, repository .html AND .htm files, crawlable internal links and, when available, verified Search Console records. Do not treat image:loc as a page. Normalize aliases and fragments without losing original URLs. Sitemap inclusion alone is not an audit of the whole site.

Each row records URL, content type, primary reader intent, current title/description/H1, canonical, HTTP, generic robots, Googlebot-specific directives, Yandex-specific directives, sitemap membership, internal links, image count/ALT status, structured-data status, source/author/review-date presence when appropriate, AEO editorial-review status, indexing-verification source/date, current action and publication state.

Required before a new public page or material update is called ready:
1. One distinct useful intent and substantive content; no mass synonym/location doorway pages and no copied full evidence pages.
2. Unique descriptive title and meta description grounded in that page, clear H1 and sensible heading structure. Do not add a meta-keywords list as an indexing tactic.
3. Correct public canonical, HTTP 200, crawlability and no unintended Google noindex; links from a relevant hub and to relevant evidence. Preserve old URLs and anchors.
4. Inclusion of intended canonical URL in the correct sitemap with truthful lastmod. Existing closed drafts, preview/staging, error pages, internal admin/bot panels and redirect aliases are NOT to be indexed just to reach a 100% counter.
5. AEO: the actual reader question is answered clearly near the relevant heading; explain terminology, identify sources and distinguish hypothesis, preprint, historical evidence and product-specific evidence. A short answer or FAQ is not a substitute for adequate substance. Do not apply a mandatory FAQ section to every page type.
6. Structured data must match visible content. Use appropriate Article/NewsArticle/BreadcrumbList/Organization/WebPage types where valid; FAQPage only for real visible questions and answers when applicable. No fabricated authors, medical reviewers, dates, ratings or efficacy. Valid JSON is not proof of rich-result eligibility.
7. Inspect each meaningful image before writing or changing its ALT. Describe what is visibly present, distinguish diagrams/illustrations from clinical photographs, and avoid keyword stuffing. Decorative images can have alt="" when appropriate. Pages without images have ALT status not_applicable. Preserve source credit, existing images, responsive sizing and intrinsic dimensions. Schema/alt metadata is not clinical evidence.
8. Post-deployment verification must check actual live URL, changed content and metadata. A preview commit or uploaded file is not deployment; HTTP200 and a sitemap are not Google indexing.

Status dimensions must remain separate: technically_crawlable, sitemap_listed, editorial_aeo_reviewed, image_alt_attribute_present, image_alt_visually_verified, google_index_verified. Unknown indexing or visual verification stays null/unknown, never passes automatically.
