Website Privacy Notice

This notice covers contact enquiries and website measurement on BHOC Therapeutics, BHOC Veterinary and BHOC Transplant.

Contact enquiries

The forms ask for your name, email address, organisation, area of interest and message. BHOC uses these details to respond to your enquiry and follow up on the conversation. Please keep patient-identifying information and confidential records out of website forms.

How enquiries reach us

The forms send your information over HTTPS to FormSubmit, which forwards enquiries to BHOC by email. FormSubmit describes its 30-day submission archive in its service documentation. You can also email info@bhoctherapeutics.com directly.

Website measurement

The sites use Ahrefs Web Analytics to understand visits and page use. See the Ahrefs Web Analytics description for details.

Questions about your information

Email info@bhoctherapeutics.com if you have a privacy question or want to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of information you shared with us.
