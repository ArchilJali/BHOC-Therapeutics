BHOC

Designed beyond red-cell blood-group barriers, intact-cell constraints and donor-blood logistics.

One essential function - oxygen transport.Not the full complex architecture of blood.
Compatible with all blood types by design.
Built for multi-species development.

BHOC separates hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport from the complex cellular architecture of donor blood. It is designed as a cell-free, plasma-phase molecular carrier for movement through the microcirculation toward the tissue level.

Unlike an intact RBC, BHOC does not depend on cell-surface blood-group antigens, crossmatching, cellular deformability, a red-cell membrane or the RBC intracellular co-factor environment. The design also reduces dependence on donor availability, cold-chain storage and inventory rotation. Oxygen loading and release remain governed by the carrier's engineered oxygen-affinity profile and local oxygen partial-pressure gradients.
