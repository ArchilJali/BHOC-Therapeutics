The Science

Science behind oxygen delivery.

Oxygen delivery is a physiological chain connecting oxygen loading, hemoglobin-mediated transport, circulation, microcirculation, tissue exchange, cellular sensing and mitochondrial metabolism.

Precision OxygenationFrom oxygen carrying to functional tissue oxygen delivery.Hemoglobin concentration and arterial oxygen saturation matter, but they do not independently establish whether enough oxygen reaches the tissue.

Physiology First

From lungs to mitochondria.

Effective oxygen delivery depends on oxygen loading, cardiac output, regional blood flow, vascular regulation, microcirculatory distribution, hemoglobin oxygen affinity, unloading, diffusion, extraction and metabolic demand.

This is why BHOC Therapeutics treats tissue oxygen delivery as the relevant functional endpoint, while avoiding the claim that a hemoglobin number alone proves physiological benefit.

01 / LOADING

Oxygen binding and affinity

The carrier must load oxygen under appropriate conditions and retain a useful, technology-specific oxygen-affinity profile.

02 / DISTRIBUTION

Flow and microcirculation

Oxygen-carrying capacity has meaning only when circulation and regional distribution support movement toward tissue.

03 / UTILIZATION

Tissue exchange and metabolism

Delivery ultimately depends on unloading, diffusion, cellular oxygen sensing and mitochondrial demand.
