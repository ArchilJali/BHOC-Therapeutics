Clinical & Translational Applications

Where oxygen delivery matters.

BHOC Therapeutics studies oxygen-critical environments in which donor-blood access, compatibility, logistics, time or physiology can limit conventional care.

Evidence BoundaryDevelopment directions, not approved treatment claims.Every application remains indication-, technology-, species- and evidence-specific.

PPH / Maternal Emergency

Postpartum hemorrhage exposes the blood-access gap.

PPH is a time-critical emergency in which bleeding control, resuscitation and access to compatible blood must converge quickly. BHOC relevance remains a research question about complementary oxygen delivery, not an approved PPH treatment claim.

Time criticalMinutes can determine organ injury and survival.
Access limitedBlood availability and logistics vary widely.

EMS / Prehospital / Remote Care

The oxygen-delivery crisis starts before the hospital.

Trauma, hemorrhage and distance create a critical interval between injury and definitive care. BHOC is evaluated as a potential bridge alongside hemorrhage control and established resuscitation pathways.

PortableDesigned for distributed emergency availability.
Donor independentDesigned beyond blood-group matching and crossmatch logistics.

Application Framework

Eight oxygen-critical development directions.

Hb

Acute & Severe Anemia

Reduced oxygen-carrying capacity or lack of suitable donor blood.

Tx

Organ Transplantation

Oxygenated organ support, viability and sustainability solutions.

O₂

Ischemia, Hypoxia & Cardiac Emergencies

Supply-demand mismatch and impaired oxygen availability to tissue.

SCD

Sickle Cell Disease & Anemia

Research context in anemia, hypoxia and transfusion support.

VET

Veterinary Medicine

Oxyglobin, animal-use literature and veterinary regulatory precedent.

RC

Remote & Resource-Limited Care

Austere, disaster, battlefield and humanitarian environments.

OR

Surgery & Bloodless Resuscitation

High blood loss, delayed transfusion or care pathways without donor blood.

PBM

Patient Blood Management

Complementary approaches intended to reduce dependence on allogeneic transfusion.
