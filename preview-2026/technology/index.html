The Technology

Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier technology.

BHOC frames hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport around one essential function rather than around replacement of blood as a whole.

BHOCBlood is a system. Oxygen delivery is a function.BHOC means Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier. The platform is positioned within the broader HBOC and oxygen-therapeutics field.

Functional Platform

One critical function, separated from red-cell complexity.

BHOC separates hemoglobin-mediated oxygen transport from the complex cellular architecture of donor blood. It is designed as a cell-free, plasma-phase molecular carrier for movement through the microcirculation toward the tissue level.

Blood performs many functions beyond oxygen transport. BHOC Therapeutics therefore does not position BHOC as artificial blood or as a replacement for donor blood.

01 / CELL-FREE

Beyond intact red-cell constraints

No red-cell membrane, cell-surface blood-group antigens or dependency on the RBC intracellular co-factor environment.

02 / AFFINITY

Engineered oxygen loading and release

Oxygen transport is governed by the carrier's engineered oxygen-affinity profile and local oxygen partial-pressure gradients.

03 / LOGISTICS

Designed for availability

Long shelf life at room temperature, an endotoxin-free specification and tolerance of routine handling and transport vibration.

Molecular Scale

Designed for microcirculation and tissue-level delivery.

Unlike an intact red blood cell, a molecular carrier does not depend on cellular deformability to move in the plasma phase through the microvascular compartment.

Historical Terminology

BHOC, HBOC, Hemopure, Oxyglobin and Biopure.

The scientific literature commonly uses HBOC for hemoglobin-based oxygen carriers. Hemopure, Oxyglobin, Biopure and HBOC-201 remain important legacy and evidence-discovery terms.
