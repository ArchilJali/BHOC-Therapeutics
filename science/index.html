The Science

BHOC Science: Oxygen Delivery, Metabolism & Physiology

Science behind oxygen delivery. Oxygen delivery is not a single number. It is a physiological chain connecting oxygen loading, hemoglobin-mediated transport, circulation, microcirculation, tissue exchange, cellular sensing and metabolism.

Physiology first

From the lungs to the mitochondria.

Hemoglobin concentration and arterial oxygen saturation are important, but they do not by themselves establish whether oxygen delivery is adequate. Blood flow, vascular regulation, microcirculatory distribution, oxygen affinity, diffusion, extraction and metabolic demand all contribute to the final biological outcome.

Lungs→Hemoglobin→Circulation→Microcirculation→Tissue→Cell→Mitochondria

Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

Blood is a system. Oxygen delivery is a function.

BHOC is evaluated within this functional framework. The relevant question is not only how much oxygen can be carried, but whether an oxygen-delivery approach is appropriate to the physiological need, clinical context and evidence available for a specific technology.
